Project Builder

Pick a structure template, then give each component as many material lines as it needs. Every line carries its own material, amount and unit. Units written as a percentage, or as litres per cubic metre, also ask for the reference amount they are a percentage of, which is what makes them mean anything.

Generating components replaces anything currently on the form.
